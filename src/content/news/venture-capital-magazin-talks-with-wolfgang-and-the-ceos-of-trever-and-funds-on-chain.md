<p>Blockchain Founders Capital and our portfolio companies Trever and Funds on Chain were featured in the 5/2024 edition of the German Venture Capital Magazin.</p>

<p>Although there has been some consolidation in the number of start-ups and financing rounds following the boom year of 2021, <strong>the mood in the sector is quite positive</strong>.</p>

<p>This is the conclusion of journalist Alexander Görbing of Venture Capital Magazin, who spoke with experts about the investment climate in the fintech sector.</p>
