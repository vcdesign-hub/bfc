<p>Bibin Babu, co-founder of Paycio, hosted a panel with Laura Inamedinova from Gate.io, Gopi Kannappan from bitsCrunch, Prakash Somosundram from AYA, and Constantin Kogan from TDX on “The Future of Finance” on “Building the Future of Finance (Payments, Market Dynamics, and Data Insights”.</p>

<p>In this panel discussion, find out<br/></p>

<ul><li>Why the current state of DeFi user adoption is low, with less than 7.8 million people staking, out of 560 million people who have some exposure to crypto</li><li>Bitcoin maximalists and Ethereum maximalists hinder collaboration that is necessary to steer the ecosystem towards a single focus</li><li>Why the current complexity of blockchain and cryptocurrency can be overwhelming for new users, who have to navigate different blockchains, wallets, and cryptocurrencies</li><li>How the use of AI agents and voice-activated transactions may help to improve the user experience and make cryptocurrency more accessible to a broader audience</li></ul>
