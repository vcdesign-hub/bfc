<p>Dear Friends &amp; Family,</p>

<p>When did you buy your first Amazon share?</p>

<p>Anecdotal evidence suggests that retail investors in Europe only began investing in tech stocks around 2015, nearly two decades after the internet became mainstream with the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=145bb02289&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">introduction of Microsoft Windows 95.</a></p>

<p>However, this initial boom was short-lived, with the sector appearing to be 'dead in the water' in the early 2000’s only to regain unprecedented relevance in our lives years later.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45379/45379-1.jpeg"/></p>

<p>Most people don't realize that the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=ded5b4f829&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">pendulum of every new technology</a> swings wildly. It's normal, and crypto is no exception.</p>

<p>Who would have thought in December 2022 that Bitcoin would reach an <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=73d6277c6d&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">all-time high</a> only 15 months later? What a difference a year makes.</p>

<p><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=4815901b0a&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Sam Bankman-Fried</a> may no longer be able to participate in the thriving crypto ecosystem, but honest and diligent innovators from Bangalore, Berlin, Barcelona, and Denver will drive the next remarkable technological shift.</p>

<p>Blockchain technology will become an integral part of our technical infrastructure and indispensable within the next two decades.</p>

<p>Similar to how open-source Linux today <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=7f8ccf3493&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">powers 96% of global servers</a> and runs on virtually every smart device, blockchains that do not belong to a corporate tech behemoth will provide the smart databases businesses will run on.</p>

<p>Resilient, safe, fast and efficient.</p>
