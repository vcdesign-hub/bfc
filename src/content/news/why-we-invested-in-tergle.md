<h4>What is it about auditing?</h4>

<p>Auditing is not glamorous. But it is vital. </p>

<p><strong>The internal audit function is the bedrock of financial integrity</strong> for growth-stage companies scaling operations, entering capital markets, or preparing for public scrutiny.</p>

<p>But audit processes today are notoriously labor-intensive and time-consuming. </p>

<p>Highly educated and expensive professionals often spend inordinate hours on mundane tasks: reconciling figures between documents, ticking and tying spreadsheets, manually selecting samples, etc. </p>

<p><strong>Auditors spend an estimated 60% of their time on administrative or document-heavy tasks</strong>. This is inefficient and contributes to talent shortages in the field. </p>

<p>Moreover, manual processes carry a risk of human error, and critical issues can be missed. These pain points create demand for automation. </p>

<p>If an AI tool can cut the rote work by even 20-30%, that’s a huge cost saving for firms and can improve staff retention by <strong>letting auditors focus on more rewarding work</strong>.</p>

<h4>How to enter a huge market with giant incumbents</h4>

<p>Tergle operates in a $ 70bn global audit industry, with internal audit alone accounting for over $ 15bn. </p>

<p>As the Big Four <a href="https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbesbusinesscouncil/2025/03/21/the-changing-nature-of-auditing-considerations-for-incorporating-ai/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">pour billions into AI platforms</a>, startups like Tergle have a <strong>particular opportunity with internal auditors</strong>.</p>

<p>The company is already showing traction, with multiple billion-dollar companies paying to automate their workflow. </p>

<p>Tergle’s go-to-market is smart: start with mid-sized tech companies facing compliance pressure but lacking audit headcount. These teams are nimble, under-tooled, and desperately need automation.</p>

<p>The long-term vision is ambitious: build a modular execution layer for audit and compliance, where AI doesn’t just assist, but delivers finished work.</p>

<h4>The challenge</h4>

<p>Overall, <strong>Tergle can be seen as a pioneer of the “augmented auditor” era</strong>, and its success will depend on continuing to deliver real results for auditors and staying ahead of both startups and incumbents in innovation. </p>

<p>The audit of the future is one where humans and AI work hand-in-hand, and Tergle is positioning itself to be a driving force in creating that future.</p>
