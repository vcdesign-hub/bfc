<p>Dear Friends &amp; Family,</p>

<p>Do you say "please" when you enter a prompt in ChatGPT? </p>

<p>Call me old school, but I often add this little word to my commands.</p>

<p><strong>Not because ChatGPT is a civilized human, but because I like to think of ourselves as a civilized humans.</strong></p>

<p>And yes, I know it costs a few extra tokens, but I find this acceptable for the sake of maintaining a respectful communication.</p>

<p>It would be sad to see people forgetting civilized language when they accidentally talk to real humans in the future.</p>

<h4>Betting against Sam Altman</h4>

<p>If you are a founder, you <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=e29e223e23&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">better watch this video</a> if you don't want to wake up one morning and <strong>discover that your product has been steamrolled</strong> by ChatGPT.</p>

<p><img alt="Description of Image" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45426/45426-1.png"/></p>

<p>Altman argues that there are fundamentally two strategies to build on Artificial Intelligence:</p>

<p>There is <strong>one strategy where you assume that AI models do not get better</strong>, and you build all these little things on top of it to make up for its shortcomings.</p>

<p>The other strategy is to <strong>assume that the AI models stay on the same level of trajectory</strong> and the models keep getting better.</p>

<p>Ninety-five percent of the world should be betting on the latter category, but many startups have been building in the former category and will be steamrolled.</p>

<p>Darwinism in its purest form.</p>

<p><strong>How do you compete against a Swiss army knife of applications with unlimited funding?</strong></p>

<p>We live in amazing times.</p>
