<p>Web3 Network and FINFOR hosted the inaugural <a href="https://web3-network.de/forum-digitale-vermoegenswerte" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Frankfurt Digital Assets Conference (FDV)</a>, where <a href="https://de.linkedin.com/in/wolfgang-maennel" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Wolfgang</a> moderated a panel on real-world blockchain applications.</p>

<p>The panel featured<br/></p>

<ul><li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexander-hoeptner-0a00962?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Alexander Höptner</strong></a><strong> (</strong><a href="https://allunity.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>AllUnity</strong></a><strong>)</strong>, who shared insights on developing a Euro-denominated stablecoin with DWS;</li><li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/volkerbraunberger?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Volker Braunberger</strong></a><strong> (</strong><a href="https://fundsonchain.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Funds on Chain</strong></a><strong>)</strong>, who presented his solution for unlocking new distribution channels and cutting administrative costs in the fund industry;</li><li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/davidriemer36?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>David Riemer</strong></a><strong> (</strong><a href="https://arttrade.io/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>arttrade</strong></a><strong>)</strong>, who explained how to make art an investable asset for family offices and asset managers;</li><li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexander-lenz-4535439?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Alexander Lenz</strong></a><strong> (</strong><a href="https://www.smape.capital/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>SMAPE Capital</strong></a><strong>)</strong>, who outlined strategies for investing in digital assets; and</li><li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/dierk?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Dierk Wilhelmsmeyer</strong></a><strong> (</strong><a href="https://www.tradevest.ai/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Tradevest</strong></a><strong>)</strong> introduced his AI-powered platform, which is designed to accelerate digital asset growth for banks and asset managers.</li></ul>

<p><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45699/45699-1.webp"/></p>

<p>At a time when many are mostly fixated on meme coins and speculative trading, these founders are using blockchain technology to build the infrastructure of the future—shaping the tech stack for decades to come.</p>

<p><a href="https://de.linkedin.com/in/wolfgang-maennel" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Wolfgang</a> was also interviewed by <a href="https://de.linkedin.com/in/henriette-schulte-9b90502a9" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Henriette Schulte</a> from <a href="https://www.fundview.de/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Fundview</a>, a leading German publisher for the fund industry. They discussed the stark differences in startup risk perception between Europe and the USA.</p>
