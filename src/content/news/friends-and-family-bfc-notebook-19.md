<p>Dear Friends &amp; Family,</p>

<p>In the <strong>race between humans and machines</strong>, a recent high-profile coding competition ended with a Polish programmer narrowly <a href="https://cybernews.com/ai-news/human-beats-open-ai-coding-contest/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">beating OpenAI’s model</a> by just 9.5%.<br/></p>

<p>I’d bet on <a href="https://polymarket.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Polymarket</a> that this has been the last time a human claimed victory.<br/></p>

<p>Billions of dollars are now being poured into a new AI coding arms race, very <strong>reminiscent of the cloud wars</strong> a decade ago.</p>

<p><a href="https://cybernews.com/ai-news/human-beats-open-ai-coding-contest/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45872/45872-1.webp"/></a></p>

<p>OpenAI resembles Amazon Web Services: the "established" leader.<br/></p>

<p>Meanwhile, Claude is shaping up to be a fast-growing contender, offering clever features similar to those of Azure or Google Cloud at the time.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=20vc" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Anthropic’s Claude</a> may not yet lead in market share, but it is <strong>arguably becoming dominant</strong> in performance and influence, particularly in developer tools.</p>

<p><strong>Billion-dollar AI wrapper companies</strong> like Lovable and Cursor are built on backbones <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4lFDEWFzHVk" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">they don’t control</a>. <a href="https://www.klover.ai/ai-strategy-windsurf-acquired-by-openai-then-google-then-cognition-ai/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Windsurf</a> learned that the hard way: a planned acquisition by OpenAI led to their Claude access <strong>being cut off overnight</strong>.</p>

<p>Such dependency would have scared investors away in the past.</p>

<p><strong>But this time, it’s different.</strong></p>

<p>The old rules of venture capital are shifting, and the <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/29/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-fresh-capital-at-170-billion-valuation.html#:~:text=Anthropic%20is%20looking%20to%20raise,Middle%20East%20for%20new%20capital." rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">capital now in play is staggering</a>.</p>

<p>When FTX collapsed in November 2022, $8 billion in customer funds vanished. But Sam Bankman-Fried <strong>had also invested in Anthropic</strong> as early as 2021.</p>

<p>Had the FTX <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/crypto-exchange-ftx-sell-shares-ai-startup-anthropic-2024-02-22/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">insolvency administrator</a> held onto this 8% stake, it would be worth $12 billion today, more than enough to compensate for the FTX trading losses.</p>

<p>AI will unlock almost infinite use cases. But <strong>the ability to build whatever you can imagine</strong> and instantly turn ideas into products is a profound game-changer.</p>

<p>We’re not quite there yet.</p>

<p>As every <a href="https://lovable.dev/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Lovable</a> vibe coder will tell you, prompt engineering still isn’t production-ready. But give it a few months, at most, a year, and that will change.</p>

<p>And maybe, just maybe, <a href="https://medium.com/data-science-collective/why-are-chinese-companies-open-sourcing-their-large-language-models-73270f8af06e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">open-source Chinese LLMs</a> will evolve so rapidly that Claude will be worth a lot less?</p>

<p>With so many things changing, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL-UtNTycGr/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">what will stay the same</a>?</p>

<p>Our conviction remains strong: <strong>technology will continue to shape and fundamentally improve human life</strong>.</p>

<p>And <strong>outstanding founders</strong>, wherever they are, will <strong>continue to build the future</strong> with relentless determination.</p>

<p>“<a href="https://www.kindo.ai/blog/will-ai-kill-saas-the-great-debate-for-cybersecurity-and-founders" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Time to copy</a>” won’t be how fast Lovable can spin up a <a href="https://javascript.plainenglish.io/i-built-copy-ai-clone-over-the-weekend-434eab640a56" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">DocuSign clone</a>.</p>

<p><strong>The real moat lies in deep domain expertise, obsessively listening to customers, building strong communities and brands, and delivering better user experiences.</strong></p>

<p>Most companies won’t build software from scratch. They’ll focus on their core competencies and assemble off-the-shelf software from modular blocks, <a href="https://unified.to/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">powered by reliable API tools</a> and shared data lakes.</p>

<p>People will be able to do so much more with so much less.</p>

<p>However, we will always need <a href="https://www.bfc.vc/portfolio" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">bold, visionary founders</a>—those who see what’s possible, know how to build it, and <strong>take the leap to make it real</strong>.</p>

<p>It’s a great time to invent.</p>

<h4>Portfolio Updates</h4>

<p>Everyone in crypto is <strong>talking about stablecoins</strong> these days.</p>

<p>Our portfolio company <a href="https://www.talentir.com/de" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Talentir</a>, based in Vienna, saw this coming early—<a href="https://www.bfc.vc/recent-news/why-we-invested-in-talentir" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">we backed them</a> in April 2023.</p>

<p>They’ve built a <strong>payout platform</strong> purpose-built for the creative industry, eliminating the administrative headaches artists and agencies typically face when managing payments.</p>

<p>Even better: users can earn interest directly on the stablecoins held in their wallets.</p>

<p>But here’s the remarkable part: Talentir does all this <strong>without ever mentioning “blockchain” or “crypto</strong>” when they talk to prospective customers.</p>

<p>They’ve introduced a level of ease of use that’s rare in the crypto world.</p>

<p>For years, people have said that blockchain adoption depends on user-friendliness.</p>

<h4>Recommended</h4>

<p>Founder stories are often <strong>romanticized and glorified</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>Scroll through social media, and you might think that every founder effortlessly makes it big.<br/></p>

<p>But even founders who make it rarely talk about the <strong>panic attacks</strong>, the <strong>sleepless nights</strong>, the <strong>relationships</strong> that <strong>crumbled</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>That’s why I was intrigued to hear of a successful founder <strong>who went through hell and back</strong>, and then decided to write about it.<br/></p>

<p>His name is <a href="https://www.xaverlehmann.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Xaver Lehmann</a>. I’ve known him since 2013, when he worked as an intern for us.<br/></p>

<p>Fast forward a few years, Xaver went on to found a chatbot company, which he <a href="https://ir.liveperson.com/news-releases/news-release-details/liveperson-acquires-e-bot7-make-self-serve-quick-launch" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">successfully exited</a> for $60 million after just five years.<br/></p>

<p>On paper, it's the perfect story, the dream outcome.<br/></p>

<p><strong>But those five years were anything but ordinary</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>You think a dog year is 7 human years? How many for a founder year?<br/></p>

<p>And <strong>the exit doesn’t magically fix the damage</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>After the successful sale of his company, Xaver didn't celebrate. He went through a long and painful recovery from total burnout.<br/></p>

<p>Few founders I know would choose anything other than building their company.<br/></p>

<p>But some forget that the journey often takes 10 years or more.<br/></p>

<p>And if you don’t <strong>take care of yourself</strong> along the way, you might not be around to enjoy the fruits of your success.<br/></p>

<p>Xaver has begun <a href="https://xaverlehmann.substack.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">publishing a series of blog posts</a> about his journey to raise awareness for this issue.<br/></p>

<p>I recently met him in Munich to get more background information on his story.</p>

<p><a href="https://xaverlehmann.substack.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45872/45872-4.webp"/></a></p>

<p>He told me he might eventually publish his founder story in a book.<br/></p>

<p>Until then, <strong>subscribe to his blog</strong> or <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/xaverlehmann/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">follow him on LinkedIn</a> to catch a new chapter every Tuesday.<br/></p>

<p>It's <strong>recommended reading for any founder in the trenches</strong> and everybody else in the start-up ecosystem.</p>
