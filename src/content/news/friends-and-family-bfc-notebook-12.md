<p>Dear Friends and Family,</p>

<p>Did you know that the first solar panel was <a href="https://www.everenewables.co.uk/post/the-history-of-solar-panels-and-who-invented-them" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">installed in 1883</a>?</p>

<p><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45502/45502-1.png"/></p>

<p>We tend to forget <strong>how long it can take</strong> for a new technology to gain traction.</p>

<p>"<strong>It is really unpredictable to say which technologies will matter</strong>" said <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-SJ-zvsALgo" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Marc Andreessen recently</a> and if I may add "<strong>and when they will matter</strong>".</p>

<p>This is what makes investing in technology so fascinating. And so tricky.</p>

<p>Knowing if a technology will prevail is not enough. <strong>You also need to know when</strong>.</p>

<p>Think of it like playing the lottery—your lucky numbers might come up, but you never know which weekend drawing will make you a winner.</p>

<p>History is full of inventors, start-ups, and investors who got it right. </p>

<p>But it's also full of countless <a href="https://www.siliconrepublic.com/innovation/da-vinci-innovation-invention-tech-gadgets-ahead-of-their-time" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">visionaries who were ahead of their time</a> and ended up empty-handed.</p>

<p><strong>Balancing foresight with timing</strong> is what venture capital investing is all about.</p>

<p>Born in India, he moved to Queens, New York, at the age of nine and went on to become one of the most respected startup investors in Silicon Valley. </p>

<p>Beyond his investment acumen, he's also known for his profound philosophical insights on life and business.</p>

<p>Ravikant's nuggets of wisdom are sure to keep you thinking long after you put down the book—perfect for a little intellectual stimulation during your downtime.</p>
