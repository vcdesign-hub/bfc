<p>Dear Friends &amp; Family,</p>

<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dario_Amodei" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Dario Amodei</a>, CEO of <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Anthropic</a>, <a href="https://futurism.com/anthropic-ceo-admits-ai-ignorance" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">recently acknowledged</a> what many in the field have long known but rarely say out loud: <strong>we don’t fully understand how today’s AI systems work</strong> - and that worries people.<br/></p>

<p>“This lack of understanding is essentially <strong>unprecedented in the history of technology</strong>."</p>

<p><a href="https://www.inc.com/kit-eaton/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-predicts-half-of-all-entry-level-office-jobs-will-disappear/91195534" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45823/45823-1.webp"/></a></p>

<p>His candid admission about the “black box” nature of AI has reignited debate across the tech world about safety and ethics in artificial intelligence.</p>

<p>It’s fascinating, and a little unsettling, that, <strong>for the first time, digital technology isn’t strictly binary</strong> (Quantum tech, of course, will bring its <a href="https://www.iotworldtoday.com/quantum/quantum-computers-evolve-beyond-binary" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">own set of nuances</a>).</p>

<p>AI feels like it has a mind of its own: sometimes brilliant, sometimes... not.</p>

<p><strong>It feels almost human</strong>.</p>

<p>Maybe we really <em>can</em> train AI toward something like consciousness after all.</p>

<p>Or maybe it’s already training itself.</p>

<p>Blockchains, in contrast, don’t suffer from this ambiguity.</p>

<p>Their logic is deterministic. Their outputs are predictable. Everything is binary.</p>

<p><strong>Ideal </strong><a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/vipinbharathan/2023/06/25/guardrails-for-ai-what-is-possible-today/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>guardrails</strong></a><strong> for the agentic AI revolution.</strong></p>

<h4><strong></strong>Welcome Tergle</h4>

<p>What do <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/adambowen03/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Adam Bowen</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/willtarr/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">William Tarr</a> from the UK have in common with Mark Zuckerberg, Steve Jobs, and Vitalik Buterin?</p>

<p>They started their company <strong>when they were just 20 and 21.</strong></p>

<p><strong></strong>While most people their age are still figuring out what to do with their lives, Adam and William are laser-focused on something that might seem boring to some but is absolutely critical to the smooth functioning of our economy: <strong>auditing</strong>.</p>

<p>With <a href="https://tergle.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Tergle</a>, they’re <strong>bringing AI to the audit industry</strong>.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.bfc.vc/recent-news/why-we-invested-in-tergle" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45823/45823-2.webp"/></a></p>

<p>Fresh out of Y Combinator, with <a href="https://monzo.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Monzo</a> founder <a href="https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/tom-blomfield-backstory" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Tom Blomfield</a> as their mentor, they’ve already landed billion-dollar companies as early clients.<br/></p>

<p>Not bad for year one.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the Big Four accounting firms <a href="https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/big-four-firms-roll-out-ai-that-can-handle-routine-tasks-solo" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">are pledging hundreds of millions</a> to build AI tools for external auditors.</p>

<p>Adam and William are starting from the inside - <strong>enabling internal audit teams to focus on high-value work</strong> by automating much of the manual checking and busywork.</p>

<p>We’re proud to back them alongside <a href="https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tergle" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Y Combinator</a>, <a href="https://www.pioneerfund.vc/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Pioneer Fund</a>, <a href="https://www.aminocapital.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Amino Capital</a>, and other top-tier investors, as they quietly build <strong>what auditors have always wanted but never knew was possible</strong>.</p>

<h4>Welcome Raremarq</h4>

<p><a href="https://www.raremarq.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Raremarq</a> was born out of a shared passion that unites millions: <strong>a deep love for physical collectibles</strong>, the creators who make them, and the stories they tell.</p>

<p>While much of the world is chasing (likely short-lived) digital assets and virtual goods, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/christopher-tung/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Chris Tung</strong></a><strong> is taking the opposite approach</strong>.</p>

<p>He believes nothing is more powerful than a tangible artifact - and the human story behind it.<br/></p>

<p><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/christopher-tung_im-so-happy-to-announce-blockchain-founders-activity-7331775049248452610-ozit/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45823/45823-3.webp"/></a></p>

<p>Chris’s journey began at 17, when he met <strong>Marvel legend </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2crfVE9QskQ" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Frank Cho</strong></a> at a comic convention.<br/></p>

<p>That wasn’t just a fan encounter. It sparked a lifelong obsession with the artistry and soul of comic books.<br/></p>

<p>Eighteen years later, he launched Raremarq, a curated marketplace for rare goods from the world’s top creators.<br/></p>

<p>In its first year, <strong>the platform has surpassed $1 million</strong> in gross merchandise value, with over 200 verified sellers and 16,000 monthly active users, achieved entirely through creator-led growth and <strong>almost zero paid acquisition</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>With <strong>blockchain</strong> (for provenance, authenticity, digital twins, and payments) and <strong>AI</strong> (for discovery, personalization, buyer/seller support, and fraud prevention), Raremarq is quietly reimagining what a modern marketplace can be.<br/></p>

<p>We’re proud to back Chris and his team - alongside <a href="https://allin.com/about/jason-calacanis" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Jason Calacanis</a> and the <a href="https://www.launch.co/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">LAUNCH</a> crew - as they build the <strong>next-generation platform for superfans and creators</strong>.</p>

<h4>Recommended</h4>

<p><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuoPiiUBcOk" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Paul Graham and Jessica Livingston</strong></a>, co-founders of <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CBYhVcO4WgI&amp;list=PL5q_lef6zVkaTY_cT1k7qFNF2TidHCe-1" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Y Combinator</a>, didn’t just build the world’s most successful startup accelerator.<br/></p>

<p>They helped define modern startup culture itself: <strong>lean, fast-moving, and relentlessly focused on user love</strong> over flashy pitch decks.<br/></p>

<p>Their mantra, “<a href="https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=3240755" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>make something people want</strong></a>” continues to shape how thousands of startups build, think, and survive.<br/></p>

<p>Equally iconic are Paul Graham’s essays, published on his <a href="https://www.paulgraham.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>gloriously outdated website</strong></a><strong>, </strong>a design probably unchanged since just after <a href="https://medium.com/@timberners_lee/marking-the-webs-35th-birthday-an-open-letter-ebb410cc7d42" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Tim Berners-Lee invented the web</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.paulgraham.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45823/45823-5.webp"/></a></p>

<p>But behind that minimalist facade lies a <strong>fountain of startup wisdom</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>It’s a crash course in building companies - <strong>distilled from the lessons of 4,000 startup investments</strong> and decades of first-hand experience.<br/></p>

<p>One essay in particular, written a decade ago, feels more relevant than ever: “<a href="https://paulgraham.com/aord.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Default Alive or Default Dead</strong></a>”.<br/></p>

<p>At a time when AI enables founders to build companies on a shoestring - <strong>and simultaneously, when reaching Series A has become harder thanks to distorted expectations (“$100M ARR in 3 months” anyone?)</strong> - Graham’s message is essential reading.<br/></p>

<p>And that’s just one gem. There are <a href="https://paulgraham.com/articles.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">dozens more</a>.<br/></p>

<p>Reading Paul Graham for venture capital <strong>is like reading </strong><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poor_Charlie%27s_Almanack" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Poor Charlie’s Almanack</strong></a><strong> for investing</strong>.<br/></p>

<p>It’s the fastest path to wisdom.</p>
