<p><strong>KINO is a streaming platform designed to disrupt the traditional studio model</strong> by offering independent developers and filmmakers data-driven packages based on the performance of their content.</p>

<p>This approach allows creators to maximize their value and reach a global audience.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.bfc.vc/recent-news/why-we-invested-in-kino" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">We invested in Daril Fannin and Austin Worrell's venture, KINO, in November 2023</a>, and we’ve been utterly impressed with what they have accomplished since then.</p>

<p><strong>Here’s an excellent video by our co-investor, ARK Investment</strong>, with Brett Winton and Nick Grous discussing the future of film and television featuring KINO.</p>
