<p>We are excited to back Carsten van Husen &amp; Team leading the seed investment round of digital games publisher<a href="https://www.eleet.games/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"> ELEET GAMES</a> together with Alexander Rösner, the founder of Gameforge AG.</p>

<p><strong>Carsten van Husen is the former CEO of Gameforge</strong> and has been a renowned thought leader in the gaming space for over 20 years. </p>

<div class="my-8 overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 shadow-soft"><div class="aspect-video w-full"><iframe allowfullscreen="" class="h-full w-full" frameborder="0" loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/nx3tCIcBUv8?start=1" title="Oathbound | MMORPG Alpha Preview - Sign Up for Closed Beta Access!"></iframe></div></div>

<p>We believe that his gaming background and community experience will be a decisive differentiator compared to many Blockchain Gaming models that have sprung up that did not have gamer founders but rather crypto technologists at their core. </p>

<p><strong>Blockchain games could become one of the first real use cases for blockchain and metaverse mass adoption.</strong> This is because they are not just connecting people but potentially changing business models by aligning the incentives of developers and players.</p>

<p><strong>However, the use of blockchain technology is not without controversy among the gaming community. </strong></p>

<p>This is why ELEET GAMES will involve passionate gamers in all steps along its “Publishing 3.0” process, thus safeguarding a fun game environment. The company plans to make crypto, in the form of tokens and NFTs, fully optional with the expectation of winning the gaming community over with the distinct advantages of decentralization offered by blockchain technology. </p>

<p><img alt="" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/44671/44671-1.webp"/></p>

<p><strong>ELEET Games wants to become the “Players’ Publisher”</strong> by combining Free-to-Play and Play-to-Earn business models. Unlike traditional publishing, ELEET thinks its players should have a say in the publishing business. In return, the company will also share the commercial success and revenues of the games it publishes with the players. </p>

<p>We couldn’t be more excited to back a proven team from the gaming industry as one of the first investors in the emerging market of blockchain-based games and digital game publishing 3.0.</p>
