<p>Our personal takeaways from the week in Davos:</p>

<p><strong>Blockchain is mainstream</strong>: There were so many events around blockchain that showed that the industry is vibrant and thriving. As with every new technology, there are teething problems, but the overall consensus was that crypto would gradually become part of the tech stack of every organization.</p>

<p><strong>India is everywhere</strong>: Not just at our event, but <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=6cfeb49d85&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">India made a strong case</a> as a key tech player to be reckoned with. The young demographics, the focus on science and math in education, and the fluency in English have already resulted in India accounting for 11% of blockchain developers worldwide.</p>

<p>We expect this number to increase strongly as the government continues to remove obstacles for Indian crypto companies and benefit from this huge pool of knowledge.</p>

<p><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=5f11e54643&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Meghdut RoyChowdhury</a>, the Chief Innovation Officer of <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=45cd38281a&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Techno India Group</a>, also attended our event. As a private educational conglomerate, his company is an example of how India works towards increasing awareness about blockchain technology.</p>

<p>With <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=21d742a2b4&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">NFTICALLY </a>and its founder <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=cf149bbaa5&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Toshendra Sharma</a>, we already have one Indian company in our portfolio and believe it won’t be the last one.</p>
