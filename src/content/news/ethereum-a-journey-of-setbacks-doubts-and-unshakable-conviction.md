<p>(by Marcel Uhlmann)</p>

<p><strong>"You were just lucky to invest in Ethereum early!</strong>"</p>

<p>I hear this all the time. My response? Yes and no.  </p>

<p>Yes, I bought Ethereum when it was still under $100. </p>

<p>But luck alone wasn’t the reason. </p>

<p>It was the result of deep conviction—conviction built over years of intense learning, relentless questioning, and the realization that Ethereum is much more than just a cryptocurrency.  </p>

<p>This conviction carried me through difficult times—times when many gave up, when doubt and uncertainty dominated the market.  </p>

<p>Holding onto Ethereum was never easy. And that’s precisely why it’s worth looking back.  </p>

<h4>Ethereum: A story of skepticism – and relentless progress</h4>

<p>Ethereum has always existed under a cloud of doubt. While Bitcoin had already secured its place as digital gold, Ethereum had to fight for legitimacy. </p>

<p>Time and again, even seasoned observers questioned its future.  </p>

<p>Here are some of the most pivotal moments where Ethereum was underestimated—only to prove the skeptics wrong time after time:  </p>

<p><strong>1. DeFi Summer 2020: "ETH won’t benefit"</strong></p>

<ul><li>During the DeFi boom of summer 2020, many claimed that all the value would flow into DeFi tokens like Aave, Compound, and SushiSwap. </li><li>The argument? *"Why hold ETH when DeFi apps provide the real utility?"*  </li><li><strong>The reality:</strong> After DeFi Summer, ETH’s price surged from around $400 to nearly $5,000—while most of those DeFi tokens have faded into obscurity.  </li></ul>

<p><strong>2. Beacon Chain &amp; Staking: "The threshold will never be met"</strong></p>

<ul><li>A major milestone was the launch of the Beacon Chain in December 2020, introducing Ethereum’s staking model. </li><li>But many doubted whether the required 524,288 ETH would be staked in time.  </li><li><strong>The reality:</strong> The Beacon Chain went live as planned on December 1, 2020. Since then, millions of ETH have been staked—and despite the ability to withdraw, the number continues to grow.  </li></ul>

<p><strong>3. EIP-1559: "Miners will block it"</strong></p>

<ul><li>Before EIP-1559, one of Ethereum’s most significant upgrades, there was widespread uncertainty. </li><li>The proposal introduced a fee-burning mechanism that reduced ETH supply—a clear disadvantage for miners.  </li><li><strong>The concern:</strong> Miners would resist or destabilize the network.  </li><li><strong>The reality:</strong> EIP-1559 launched on August 5, 2021. Since then, billions of dollars worth of ETH have been burned, reducing supply and reinforcing ETH’s long-term value.  </li></ul>

<p><strong>4. The Merge: "Too complex, it will fail"</strong></p>

<ul><li>The transition from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake was Ethereum’s most ambitious technical overhaul—and one of the most significant upgrades in blockchain history.  </li><li>Many doubted the transition would go smoothly. Critics warned that miners would sabotage the process or disrupt the network.  </li><li><strong>The reality:</strong> On September 15, 2022, The Merge was executed seamlessly. Ethereum reduced its energy consumption by over 99% and laid the groundwork for future scalability.  </li></ul>

<p>Why I still believe in Ethereum</p>

<p>It would be easy to say, <strong>"See? All those doubts were unfounded."</strong> But that’s not my point.  </p>

<p>Ethereum has proven that it’s not just a visionary network—it’s a network that delivers. Step by step. Year after year.  </p>

<p>I’m not an Ethereum maximalist. I appreciate innovation in other networks, too—competition drives progress. </p>

<p>But looking back over the past seven years, I keep arriving at the same conclusion:  </p>

<h4>Ethereum delivers</h4>

<p>In an industry where many projects overpromise and underdeliver, Ethereum stands apart in one critical way: execution.  </p>

<h4>Patience pays off</h4>

<p>It’s easy to get caught up in short-term market swings. Euphoria turns to panic overnight. </p>

<p>But if you let headlines and social media dictate your decisions, you’ll quickly lose sight of the bigger picture. </p>

<p>This isn’t just true for crypto—it applies to every investment.  </p>

<p>Many people underestimate the importance of truly understanding a topic instead of just skimming the surface. </p>

<p>I’ve seen people enter crypto through me, and within weeks, they’re trying to give me investment advice.  </p>

<p>That tells me one thing: <strong>People often underestimate the value of experience.</strong></p>

<p>Watching ten YouTube videos doesn’t make you an expert. There’s a fundamental difference between knowledge and experience.  </p>

<p>Experience means weathering downturns. It means making sound decisions even when the market moves against you. </p>

<p>It means staying level-headed while others panic.  </p>

<h4>Final thought: Luck or conviction?</h4>

<p>Was I lucky to buy Ethereum at $80? Maybe.  </p>

<p>But it wasn’t luck that made me hold onto it when the price crashed. </p>

<p>It wasn’t luck that kept me believing in Ethereum when many declared it dead. </p>

<p>It wasn’t luck that made me dive deep into its technology.  </p>

<p>It was conviction.  </p>

<p>And today, that conviction is stronger than ever.  </p>

<p>Ethereum isn’t perfect—but it’s the strongest foundation in the decentralized world. And that’s why I remain convinced that Ethereum will play a central role in the future of the internet.  </p>

<p>The past seven years have proven one thing: <strong>Ethereum doesn’t just promise—it delivers.</strong></p>
