<p>Dear Friends and Family,</p>

<p><strong>“Protocols”.</strong></p>

<p>That is the answer to the question, <strong>"What has been a fundamental driver of technological progress over the past fifty years?"</strong></p>

<p>Every protocol serves a specific function.</p>

<p>TCP/IP has enabled the linking up of computers in a network, HTTP allowed the creation of the internet, SSL/TLS made it secure for payment systems, Blockchains enabled the first cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, and Smart Contracts birthed the development of a shared ledger like Ethereum which will drive efficiency gains, new products and services.</p>

<p><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=9a92ba2dd8&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Each protocol has created trillions in value</a>, moved us forward, and allowed collaboration on an unprecedented scale.</p>

<p><strong>And yet, it still feels like we are just getting started. </strong></p>

<h4>Token2049 in Singapore</h4>

<p>In September, the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=b129d79f4c&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Token2049</a> conference drew a crowd of ten thousand attendees to Singapore, and Sagar and I were among them.</p>

<p>We had a packed week of meetings with founders in real life, comparing notes with venture capital colleagues, and feeling the current energy level in the Web3 space at numerous events.</p>

<p>Preceding the conference, we participated in the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=c68a7eeb75&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">ETH Singapore Hackathon</a>, with many side events and opportunities to meet industry movers and shakers like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=35b3623b51&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Vitalik Buterin</a>, the famous brain behind Ethereum.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45224/45224-1.jpg"/></p>

<p>We learned that <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1d69dccd45&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Japan is determined</a> to become a trailblazer in the Web3 industry and is putting regulations and incentives in place to lure founders and capital to the country. There is a strong believe that <strong>the country has missed the internet</strong> and wants to avoid making the same mistake twice.</p>

<p>Singapore, of course, is also a hub that is trying to attract Web3 businesses that require a stable regulatory framework, <strong>something that the US is unable to provide at this point.</strong></p>

<p>Asia has indeed a chance to become a strong region in everything crypto/Web3 as they are very open to new ideas and technologies and tend to implement fast with few barriers to change.</p>

<p>It has never been wise to underestimate the US. Still, it is likely that it will take until after the presidential elections in 2024 <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1ac98917cf&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">to define a crypto regulatory framework</a> like Europe now has with MiCa.</p>

<p>For us, it was a great outcome to establish a network with Asian venture capital colleagues to share deal flow and thoughts about the industry.</p>

<h4>Crypto Asset Conference 2023 - The October Edition</h4>

<p>Last week, it was “homecoming weekend” at the Frankfurt School <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=5c05345092&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Crypto Asset Conference</a>.</p>

<p>Although there may not have been major new announcements, <strong>progress comes in small steps.</strong></p>

<p>For me, one of the most impressive projects was <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=9be6223ff9&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Volker Braunberger's</a> report on how his company, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1799d366d4&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Funds on Chain</a> and Frankfurt-based <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=6c3d7caf24&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Cashlink</a>, conducted the <strong>first transfer of crypto funds units</strong> with <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=06dec35805&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Metzler Asset Management</a> in accordance with the German Capital Investment Code (KAGB), the Electronic Securities Act (eWpG) and the Regulation on Crypto Fund Units (KryptoFAV).</p>

<p>Yes, it takes a lot to be compliant in the crypto world!</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45224/45224-3.jpg"/></p>

<p><strong>This paves the way for the future of the fund industry</strong> and is a key milestone in no small way. </p>

<p>It came after more than three years of developing a solution with an expert group composed of the who-is-who of the international fund industry.</p>

<p>In the end, crypto is a set of protocols that create a way to exchange value, like the Internet created a way to exchange information.</p>

<p>The still surprisingly manual and often paper (and fax!)-based investment fund industry is an <strong>ideal use case of blockchain technology.</strong></p>

<p>Unsurprisingly, Funds on Chain made it on the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=678fabd4ae&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">FinTech Germany Award 2023 shortlist</a>, recognizing the best FinTech startups in Germany. </p>

<p><strong>Fingers crossed, Volker</strong>, you are unquestionably a founder driven by a strong sense of mission!</p>

<h4>Recommended</h4>

<p>As I couldn't decide which one to pick, I am giving you not one but <strong>three recommendations</strong>.</p>

<p>As the daylight savings changeover approaches this weekend, you might find yourself with more opportunities to read and watch anyway.</p>

<p>I have always been a big fan of Michael Lewis, author of Liar's Poker and Moneyball. His most recent book "<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=22d1d02952&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Going Infinite</a>" on Sam Bankman-Fried might not answer the question whether he is <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=885f55fe1e&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">guilty or not</a>, but it is a <strong>most entertaining story about his character</strong> and the mess he got himself into with FTX.</p>

<p>Then there’s Marc Andreessen, the man who gave us the first web browser and who recently penned his <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=189c92a056&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Techno-Optimist Manifesto</a>. While you may not agree with everything he writes, it provides plenty of <strong>food for thought</strong> and makes for great conversation topics.</p>

<p>And while Artificial Intelligence <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=10261425a9&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">may not be an easy space to make direct investments</a> in, it will fundamentally leverage developers, a scarce resource. </p>

<p>In fact, after watching the “<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=f389a3e211&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">1000x Developer</a>” video, one could argue that all it takes in a startup is one experienced developer and a suite of software AI tools like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d9a978d34c&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Replit </a>or <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=f220b38f45&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">GitHub Copilot</a> to code in seconds, which previously took weeks.</p>

<p>The consequence of these tools is that a startup can be <strong>even more capital-efficient</strong> and swift than ever before.</p>

<p>As cloud computing removes the need to own hardware, <strong>AI software tools reduce the need for large developer teams. </strong></p>

<p>We anticipate that the <strong>repercussions of AI-assisted coding</strong> will be <strong>nothing short of profound.</strong></p>

<p>Isn’t it ironic how our initial reflex was that AI would primarily take over blue-collar jobs, but now it's coming after the well-paid jobs of creators and coders?</p>
