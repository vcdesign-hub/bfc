<p>Just as the rise of e-commerce, propelled by internet technology, was highly predictable, <strong>the financial system will primarily be based on blockchain technology</strong> within the next decade or two.</p>

<p>This shift will enable new forms of trading, investing, and exchange, <strong>rendering the use of proprietary company databases to track units of value obsolete</strong>.</p>

<p>Initially slow, <strong>this transformation will eventually become the new normal</strong>.</p>

<h4>The Company and Founders</h4>

<p>Trever, established in 2019 in Graz, Austria, provides financial institutions with <strong>software for managing digital assets</strong>. </p>

<p>The system covers trading, treasury management, and bookkeeping through comprehensive integrations.</p>

<p>Founded by <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/hans-juergen-griesbacher/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Hans-Jürgen Griesbacher</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/bnyra/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Benjamin Rath</a>, Trever's platform addresses the challenges traditional banking providers face when adapting to the rapidly evolving world of digital assets.</p>

<h4>The Problem</h4>

<p>Today, <strong>most financial institutions cannot offer digital assets</strong> because of their legacy systems.</p>

<p><strong>Current systems lack essential functionality</strong>, have insufficient connectivity for integration, or do not guarantee 24/7 availability. </p>

<p>This is where <strong>Trever’s Digital Asset Operating System</strong> comes in.</p>

<h4>The Solution</h4>

<p>Trever provides a compatible infrastructure that enables <strong>trading, transfer, and bookkeeping of digital assets</strong>. </p>

<p>Their solution <strong>can be fully integrated through standardized API interfaces</strong>, ensuring seamless integration with legacy systems. </p>

<p>Due to the numerous connected venues, the system is <strong>agnostic and independent</strong>. </p>

<p>This allows banks to freely choose their trading venues and trusted partners and thus remain <strong>independent</strong>.</p>

<p>Liquidity management and best execution functionality create advantages in risk management and allow competitive pricing across all trading centers.</p>

<h4>Proof of Concept and Achievements</h4>

<p>Numerous leading European financial institutions, such as V-Bank, Futurum Bank, and Bankhaus Scheich, have relied on Trever’s robust, intuitive, and compliant software for years. </p>

<p>These collaborations highlight the platform's effectiveness and ability to meet the finance industry's needs.</p>

<h4>Market Potential and Future Outlook</h4>

<p>The digital asset market is <strong>experiencing substantial growth</strong>, increasing market capitalization and transaction volumes. </p>

<p>Events like Bitcoin and Ethereum ETF approvals and partnerships between major banks and cryptocurrency platforms emphasize the mainstream adoption of digital assets. </p>

<p>The demand for solutions like Trever's will only grow in this evolving financial landscape.</p>
