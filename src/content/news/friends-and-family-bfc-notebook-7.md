<p>Dear Friends and Family,</p>

<p>Every time Sam Altman, the CEO of OpenAI, speaks, <strong>a few hundred startups die.</strong></p>

<p>His <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=eb79db5b10&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">presentations</a>, lasting just an hour, dramatically reshape the landscape, causing startups worth millions or even billions to reevaluate their competitive position.</p>

<p>ChatGPT has become the <strong>Swiss army knife of software</strong> seamlessly replacing an array of specialized applications.</p>

<p>It reminds us of the number of devices that the <strong>Apple iPhone</strong> <a href="">has replaced</a>: Telephones, telefaxes, scanners, Rolodex, directories, typewriters, calendars, calculators, alarm clocks, dictaphones, cameras, CD players, and the list goes on and on.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45278/45278-1.jpg"/></p>

<p>Watching the value of startups, nurtured over many years, reduced to rubble overnight, is <strong>brutal</strong>.</p>

<p>Both investors and founders will deal with it in their own ways navigating through their <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1f96707a0e&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">emotional cycle of change.</a></p>

<p>Unfortunately, for many, the outcome is a <strong>hard-hitting wave of write-offs</strong>, especially impacting older venture capital portfolios.</p>

<p>At the same time, the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=faad53ba44&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">power of AI in coding</a> is revolutionizing software development. </p>

<p>Small teams, comprising just two or three skilled developers, can now <strong>craft complex applications in weeks</strong> – doing what previously required large teams and years of efforts.</p>

<p>This shift brings "<strong>tech defensibility</strong>" to the forefront for startups and investors alike.</p>

<p>Understanding barriers to entry, intellectual property, industry expertise, and the impact of AI on business models becomes critical.</p>

<p>Our colleagues at <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=b834d23310&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Angular Ventures</a> recently wrote, "The factors that define these attributes are shifting before our eyes. Much of our energy over the next year will be devoted to <strong>understanding the new contours of technology defensibility</strong> in the era of AI." </p>

<p>We couldn't agree more.</p>

<p>In this context, our focus on a deep, <strong>industry-specific understanding</strong> in the founder team – as opposed to a purely technology-centric approach – is <strong>more relevant than ever</strong>.</p>

<h4>Coming to Hollywood</h4>

<p>Every so often, we encounter a founder team that raises our expectations of what <strong>excellent product-founder fit</strong> looks like. </p>

<p>Today, we're <strong>thrilled to introduce</strong> you to such a team: <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=fbe556e2f7&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Daril Fannin</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=014b79b343&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Austin Worrell</a>, the dynamic duo behind our <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=96f81036b8&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">latest investment, KINO.</a></p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img" class="mx-auto w-full max-w-full rounded-2xl lg:w-[70%]" loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45278/45278-2.jpg"/></p>

<p>The glitz and glamour of Hollywood often overshadow the gritty reality of film and TV production.</p>

<p>In so many ways, <strong>creating a movie or series is akin to starting a garage-based company</strong>, complete with similar challenges and high failure rates. </p>

<p>It's a <strong>marathon</strong> of fundraising, pre-production, production, post-production, and marketing — each stage is as critical as the last.</p>

<p>The film industry, often perceived as an <strong>exclusive club dominated by major studios</strong>, has long set the rules of the game. </p>

<p>KINO is here to <strong>challenge this status quo</strong>. </p>

<p>By providing <strong>more factual data for price negotiations</strong> and creating a unique platform that <strong>brings superfans closer</strong> to the creative process and talent, KINO aims to rebalance the scales of power.</p>

<p><strong>Blockchain technology is at the heart</strong> of KINO's strategy. It will enable fans to invest in movies early, help reduce filmmaking costs, and open up several other areas that are yet to be announced.</p>

<p>The company is already generating revenue and is <strong>backed by an A-list of co-investors</strong>, including Sequoia Capital, Slow Ventures, Blockchain Founders Fund, Genius Ventures, MetaWeb Ventures, NEAR Protocol, and more to come.</p>

<p>We are very glad to add our Web3 know-how to this stellar list of partners and look forward to every time Daril and Austin shout, "<strong>Lights, Camera, Action!</strong>"</p>

<h4>Recommended</h4>

<p>If you have a <strong>spare moment during the holidays</strong>, consider watching <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d00c55c05b&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Sam Lessin's</a> video "<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d590b1b7f2&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">The Factory System Era of Venture is Dead</a>".</p>

<p>In this video, Sam presents a compelling argument for a <strong>more sustainable approach</strong> to business growth. </p>

<p>He advocates for a slower, more deliberate expansion strategy that <strong>benefits both founders and investors</strong> in the long run. </p>

<p>His perspective is exemplified by companies like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=c9bc93dcef&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Zapier</a>, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=97ac7eaa1c&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Backblaze</a>, and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d8009fcd49&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Squarespace</a>, which <strong>predominantly bootstrapped</strong> their operations before opting for external funding.</p>

<p>The "capital light strategy” that Sam describes is also one that we look out for in startups because "<strong>just because someone gives you money, it doesn’t mean that you are going to grow faster</strong>".</p>
