<p><strong><em>"Half the money I spend on advertising is wasted; the trouble is I don't know which half."</em></strong></p>

<p>- John Wanamaker, a US merchant, over a century ago</p>

<p><strong><em></em></strong>Regarded as one of the pioneers of modern advertising, Wanamaker was notable for his then-innovative approach to retail promotion.</p>

<p><strong><em></em></strong>His observation sparked a global industry focused on solving this exact problem. An industry now worth $6 billion and expected to triple in size over the next decade.</p>

<p><strong><em></em></strong>This explosive growth is fueled by a convergence of technological innovation, economic shifts, regulatory pressures, and evolving user behavior. </p>

<p><strong><em></em></strong>Yet, measuring advertising effectiveness—especially in the mobile app space—has never been more challenging.</p>

<h4>The Attribution Gap</h4>

<p>Privacy-first implementations have disrupted long-standing measurement models. In particular, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and SKAdNetwork (SKAN) have rendered traditional mobile measurement tools increasingly unreliable.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucas-moscon/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Lucas Moscon</strong></a>, an Argentinian app builder, recognized that incumbent analytics providers were struggling to adapt due to their legacy architectures. </p>

<p>In response, he began building an AI-powered internal attribution system designed explicitly for mobile advertisers.</p>

<p>Early tests showed promising results. Lucas soon joined Entrepreneur First, where he met his technical co-founder, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/tom-darmon/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Tom Darmon</strong></a>, a machine learning engineer based in Paris.</p>

<p>Together, they raised a seed round from industry insiders who immediately grasped the significance of their work.</p>

<p>In a world where traditional ad tracking is breaking down, <strong>Appstack</strong> provides a privacy-first solution that enables mobile marketers to understand what’s working without compromising user privacy.</p>

<h4>Attribution in a Privacy-First World</h4>

<p>Mobile advertising has long promised a simple equation: spend money, acquire users, analyze what works, and double down. But this equation has become increasingly difficult to solve.</p>

<p>With the advent of ATT and SKAN, legacy mobile measurement providers (MMPs)—built around persistent device identifiers—have struggled to maintain effectiveness in today’s privacy-centric landscape.</p>

<p>Appstack reimagines attribution from the ground up. The platform connects campaign spend to in-app outcomes using <strong>anonymized, probabilistic data</strong>—completely sidestepping the need for SDKs, fingerprinting, or personal identifiers.</p>

<p>More than technically advanced, Appstack is also refreshingly easy to adopt. </p>

<p>It integrates seamlessly with tools like Firebase and Segment, providing a transparent timeline of which campaigns drove which results, complete with confidence scores and decision logic.</p>

<h4>Why We Invested</h4>

<p>Our decision to back Appstack is rooted in three key convictions:</p>

<ul><li><strong>The old model is collapsing. </strong>Regulatory and platform changes are rendering deterministic tracking obsolete. Appstack isn’t adapting to this reality—it’s built for it.</li><li><strong>Modern mobile teams are underserved. </strong>The rise of solo developers and no-code tools has created lean teams launching apps without the resources to manage complex MMPs. Appstack’s SDK-free, plug-and-play approach meets these developers where they are.</li><li><strong>The founders are exceptional. </strong>CEO Lucas Moscon brings deep domain expertise from building attribution tools at a top-50 iOS app. CTO Tom Darmon adds ML and real-time data processing skills. Together, they’ve moved with uncommon speed, securing early paying customers and strategic agency partnerships.</li></ul>

<h4>Early Traction &amp; Road Ahead</h4>

<p>Launched in 2024, Appstack is already powering campaigns for <strong>nine agencies</strong> across Europe and Latin America. Over <strong>250 apps</strong> have been pre-registered, and the team is experiencing strong inbound demand.</p>

<p>The iOS product is live, with Android support and a redesigned marketer-facing frontend launching soon. Upcoming features include cohort reporting, creative-level insights, and A/B test overlays.</p>

<p>In the longer term, Appstack aims to evolve into a comprehensive performance measurement platform, consolidating fragmented analytics tools into a unified, privacy-compliant stack.</p>

<h4>Building the Attribution Layer for a Privacy-First Internet</h4>

<p>The next generation of mobile infrastructure is being built now. Appstack is uniquely positioned at the crossroads of platform shifts, regulatory change, and developer needs.</p>

<p>Appstack has the potential to become the <strong>default attribution layer</strong> for the privacy-first internet. </p>
