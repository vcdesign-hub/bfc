<p><strong>Many people were lured to the crypto space with the promise of 100x on their investment</strong>.</p>

<p>But now that the token craze of 2014 to mid-2018 has run dry, and Bitcoin not really moving for some time now, the question I often get is, What is the Most Promising Way to Invest in the Web3 Revolution?</p>

<p>On this panel , we discussed the <strong>risks, rewards, and strategies</strong> for making the most of this space, especially with regard to <strong>Tokens, ETFs, Funds, and More</strong>.</p>

<p>Enjoy the debate!</p>
