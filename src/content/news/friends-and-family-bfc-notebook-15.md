<p>Dear Friends &amp; Family,</p>

<p>The difference between a <strong>crazy person and a genius</strong>?<br/></p>

<p>"<strong>Success</strong>" said Javier Milei recently <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1f6f47a4c5&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">on Lex Fridman's podcast</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8NLzc9kobDk" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45643/45643-1.webp"/></a></p>

<p><strong>The world could use more crazy people</strong>, the ones who turn into geniuses in the public eye when their ideas start to work.</p>

<p>2025 might require a <strong>rethinking of many </strong><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=fafe8d09bf&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>preconceived notions</strong></a>.</p>

<p>The older I get, the more I value people being open to ideas, no matter how “radical,” “socialist,” or “vulture capitalistic” they may seem.</p>

<p>Labels don’t help, they just provoke unnecessary battles. What we need is a <strong>battle of better ideas</strong>.</p>

<p>Believing that the best ideas always win is certainly naive.</p>

<p>But <strong>continuing to do the same thing over and over and</strong> expecting different results? That’s insanity—<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=8dd380886b&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">whether Einstein said it or not</a>.</p>

<p>Half a year ago, the tech industry, once proudly democratic, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=5fbe54b86f&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">started rolling out the red carpet </a>for Trump and his “Vice President,” Elon Musk.</p>

<p>Like it or not, there’s a feeling of <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=2f639ea91e&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>Aufbruchstimmung</strong></a> in the US, a fresh start, that we haven’t seen in a while.</p>

<p>If Elon Musk can launch 7,000 satellites for a global internet network or build a reusable spacecraft, <strong>why not fund him a moonshot for </strong><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=4bd2df6001&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>laser fusion</strong></a><strong>?</strong></p>

<p>The <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=41778ac696&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">International Space Station</a>, a $150 billion collaboration involving 15 countries, is probably humanity’s largest joint endeavor to date.</p>

<p><strong>Could we pull off something like that to produce abundant, cheap, and clean energy today?</strong></p>

<p>When <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=cddd3964fc&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Dogecoin</a> has a market cap of $60 billion and meme coins like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=c6569dca35&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Fartcoin</a> are nearing a billion, it’s clear that <strong>money isn’t the obstacle</strong>.</p>

<p>We need more bold and naive builders, who are <strong>chasing the one-in-a-million shot</strong> that could have a meaningful impact on the world.</p>

<p>The problem <strong>is not money, it is ambition</strong>.</p>

<h4>Welcome Bayesline</h4>

<p><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=4606dc61f1&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Thomas Bayes</a> was an 18th century English statistician and philosopher best known for formulating the "Bayes' Theorem", which provides a way to <strong>update probabilities based on new evidence</strong>.</p>

<p>That is exactly what <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=ff6f714d00&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Misha van Beek</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=c63b2a94ec&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Sebastian Janisch</a> are building with <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=467b7c2e7a&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Bayesline</a>: risk management software <strong>that operates at the speed of markets</strong>, not at the speed of humans.</p>

<p><a href="https://bayesline.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45643/45643-2.jpg"/></a></p>

<p><a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=24a58c13b5&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">We’re thrilled to be backing</a> these two exceptional founders.</p>

<p>Both have stellar track records at <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=8a26ba1a03&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">BlackRock</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=22834cf747&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Bloomberg</a>, and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=aaa0c1ffac&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">judging by the buzz on LinkedIn</a>, they have plenty of cheerleaders in the industry rooting for their success.</p>

<p>Their work reminds me of the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=583167a988&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>transformative introduction of spreadsheets</strong></a> in the 1980s with Lotus 1-2-3 (for Gen Z: Microsoft Excel), which revolutionized financial planning and modeling.</p>

<p>We’re confident Misha and Sebastian will redefine risk management with AI, creating tools as <strong>groundbreaking</strong> as spreadsheets once were.</p>

<h4>Welcome Level</h4>

<p>In today’s volatile world, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=aa53d59a0d&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">stablecoins</a> have become the <strong>foundation of the crypto ecosystem</strong>, bridging stability and innovation.</p>

<p>While many are still searching for crypto’s elusive killer app, stablecoins like Tether and USDC have quietly amassed a combined market cap exceeding $200 billion.</p>

<p>But there’s a drawback: stablecoins, as essential as they are, struggle to generate meaningful returns.</p>

<p>Enter <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=0d3a1a4ec0&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Kedian Sun</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=bc7aaba3dc&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">David Lee</a>. With exceptional credentials and deep industry expertise, they’ve built <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=e970c95199&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Level</a>, <strong>a protocol making stablecoins work harder</strong>.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.level.money/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Description of Image"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45643/45643-3.webp"/></a></p>

<p>Level issues <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=2e638563a8&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Level USD (LvIUSD)</a>, the first stablecoin backed by dollar-denominated tokens re-staked on shared security protocols like EigenLayer, Symbiotic, and Karak.</p>

<p>This dual-yield strategy not only enables holders to earn through lending but also <strong>introduces a second layer of returns via re-staking</strong>.</p>

<p>By optimizing income potential while relying on the stability of dollar-backed assets, Level is breaking new ground in a quickly expanding market.</p>

<p>We’re excited to welcome Level as <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=3cbc7e712d&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>investment #16</strong></a> in our portfolio of great founders.</p>
