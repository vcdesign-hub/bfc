<h4>OpenQ</h4>

<p>In Berlin, we invested in <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=638cc186b6&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><strong>OpenQ</strong></a>, founded by <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1782de6cbb&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Riccardo Lamanna</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=ad33bbc6c2&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Andrew O'Brien</a>, joining Speedinvest (A) and Kraken Ventures (USA).</p>

<p>OpenQ provides a cleverly designed (and, of course, AI-enhanced) <strong>Developer Relationship Management</strong> (DRM) platform that transforms how companies interact with their developer communities.</p>

<p>If you've seen how Apple (<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=f6823319b8&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">WWDC</a>), Google (<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=9c30a001d4&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Google I/O</a>), Microsoft (<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d7c7f66f96&amp;e=5e776130b3" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Microsoft Build</a>), and all blockchains <strong>compete for developer attention</strong>, you know <strong>how crucial it is for a technology platform to manage relationships with developers</strong> <strong>actively</strong>.</p>

<p>OpenQ is the tool that makes this process much more efficient.</p>
