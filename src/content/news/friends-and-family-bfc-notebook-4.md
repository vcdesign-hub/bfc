<h4>Crypto Investor Days in Hamburg</h4>

<p><strong>"Do you want to trade crypto or become an early investor in a household name?" </strong></p>

<p>was the question we asked at the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=2420e479a4&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Hamburg Crypto Investor Days</a> in the famous venue of <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1e1f576e58&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Donner &amp; Reuschel</a> at the Hamburg Binnenalster on June 21.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45119/45119-2.jpg"/></p>

<p>This event aimed to provide potential investors with insights into the current blockchain landscape, enabling them to make informed investment decisions.</p>

<p>Of course, we are slightly biased here as Blockchain Founders Capital focuses on great builders who want their products to become household names. </p>

<p><a href="">Christoph Fröhlich</a>, Editor-in-Chief at "Das Investment" <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=14840d9781&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">has the story here</a>.</p>

<p>When we talk to investors, we often find ourselves explaining the differences between blockchain, Web3, and crypto, addressing concerns about the optimal timing, the potential for excessive hype, and whether investing in AI may be a wiser choice (spoiler alert: we firmly believe that AI and blockchain is a potent mix).</p>

<p>However, the same questions were asked when computers were invented (with skeptics asserting that the world wouldn't require more than a few), when the internet was dismissed as a passing fad during the dotcom crash in 2000, and when Steve Ballmer derided the unveiling of the iPhone in 2007. </p>

<p>Even Artificial Intelligence has perpetually been deemed 'just around the corner' for the past three decades.</p>

<p>The founders we meet in the field every day understand the necessity of buildiing with numerous unknowns and persisting amidst years of uncertainty until their “overnight success” (typically taking a minimum of five years of hard work) materializes.</p>

<p>As Steve Jobs famously stated <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=1962a555f5&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">in one of the most iconic advertising videos of all time</a>: “<strong>you can disagree with them, glorify or vilify them, but the only thing you can't do is ignore them because they change things... they push the human race forward</strong>”.</p>

<h4>Portfolio Update</h4>

<p>Speaking of people who are crazy enough to think that they can change the world: </p>

<p>We made a <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=77bc9d5936&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">new investment</a> in a remarkable team and are mighty proud of these guys!</p>

<p>Let me explain:</p>

<p>Web3 technology has unlocked the potential for <strong>tokenizing items of smaller value</strong>.</p>

<p>You can buy company shares and trade them on the stock exchange, but the costs and complexities of transferring ownership rights are too big a burden for smaller value items.</p>

<p>Consider, for instance, the desire to invest in the creative output of a music artist. </p>

<p>Currently, there is no straightforward method to do so. </p>

<p>Historically, the financing of artists has primarily been the business of major labels or, in earlier times, relied upon the patronage of wealthy aristocrats supporting the likes of Mozart and Beethoven.</p>

<p><strong>This dynamic has often resulted in significant challenges in sustaining artists</strong>. After all, without adequate financing, art cannot flourish.</p>

<p>Recognizing this issue, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=4c326471ac&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Michael Höweler</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=eb9abbc592&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Alexander Franck</a>, during their tenure as managers at Amazon Music, acknowledged the transformative potential of blockchain technologies.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45119/45119-3.jpg"/></p>

<p>Inspired by these new possibilities, they conceived a platform enabling fans to invest directly in artists, providing support while also reaping the rewards of their successes.</p>

<p>Together, they founded <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=0d8b96652d&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Biddz</a>, a venture we envision becoming synonymous with music ownership in the future.</p>

<p>We thank our fellow investors, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=39562f123f&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Martin Junker</a> from <a href="">Ventures Stars</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=a5f7fbeba6&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Robert Kusche</a> from <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=a74152fd22&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">IBB Ventures</a>, who led this deal.</p>

<h4>Why our blockchain investment focus is different</h4>

<p>Our investment thesis centers around blockchain technologies, yet we are the first to say that “<strong>no one cares about Web3 or blockchain</strong>”.</p>

<p>The only thing that matters is how it can improve a current business 10x or create a new business model.</p>

<p>Like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=998e28d075&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Biddz</a>, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=191f7f1dce&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Talentir</a>, <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=a0a17f49f4&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Gameboard</a>, and the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=23b05f88ab&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">other companies we have invested in</a>.</p>

<p>It’s not about onboarding people to Web3. It’s about the seamless integration of blockchain technologies where the users already are.</p>

<p><strong>You don't have to believe in Bitcoin</strong> or bored apes to invest in companies that use blockchain/Web3 technologies. Blockchain is about so much more. </p>

<p>It is a pillar technology that will drive efficiency improvements and paradigm changes in many industries. </p>

<p>The people who can best evaluate such an impact are the ones who have been working in a particular industry for a long time and possess a profound understanding of its underlying principles.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45119/45119-5.png"/></p>

<p>The potential of blockchain is often distorted by short-term greed and misinformation, which earned the technology somewhat of a <strong>perception problem</strong>. </p>

<p>But this was also the case when the number of railway companies peaked in the United States 107 years ago and when the <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=6607a0fa23&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Neuer Markt</a> (the now-defunct German stock exchange for young companies) saw crazy valuations for companies without an actual business model in the 2000s.</p>

<p><strong>Technology is the defining theme of our time</strong>, and it will only become more crucial. And Blockchain is an integral part of this tech puzzle.</p>
