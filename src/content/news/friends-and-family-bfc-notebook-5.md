<p>Dear Friends and Family,</p>

<p>You may not know this, but <strong>Germany has played an important role in the history of Ethereum.</strong></p>

<p>The Genesis block, the first block of the Ethereum blockchain, was mined (those were the days of proof-of-work) on a Berlin Ethereum node on <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=78d48a92ab&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">July 30, 2015</a>, marking the official launch of the Ethereum network's Frontier phase.</p>

<p>Today, there are over 14,197 Ethereum nodes worldwide. According to <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=b8c09a5541&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Eth2 Nodewatch</a>, <strong>no country has deployed more nodes than Germany</strong>, currently counting 4,977.</p>

<p>Who would have thought?</p>

<p>Berlin has hosted a handful of ETH events, but Munich, the city that prides itself on being at the forefront of the digital revolution, has never had one.</p>

<p>Our Indian colleague <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=09ab35cfd8&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Sagar</a>, a self-declared Bavarian <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=b5fb36bb55&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Lederhosenträger</a> since 2013, decided <strong>that this could not stand.</strong></p>

<h4>Gamescom</h4>

<p>With already three portfolio companies in the Gaming space under our belt, Ben (our uncontested gamer-in-chief) and I ("Microsoft Solitaire" - gaming level apprentice) took a trip to Cologne to attend <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=b67a94590d&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Gamescom 2023</a>.</p>

<p>Gamescom is the world's largest gaming trade show, with over 300,000 visitors and more than 1,000 exhibitors from 100 countries.</p>

<p>A must-go event for every gamer.</p>

<p>Our key objective was to <strong>understand the state of Web3 in gaming</strong> and meet the founders of our current and potentially future portfolio companies.</p>

<p><strong>The early days of Web3 gaming were characterized by a number of poorly made games that focused solely on the speculative crypto side, with little regard for gameplay quality.</strong></p>

<p>The <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=770e61dfd5&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">rise and fall of Axie Infinity</a> is a prime example of the lack of sustainable product-market fit.</p>

<p>To understand the potential of Web3 in gaming, it helps to take a step back and understand what blockchains are all about.</p>

<p>At the core, they are a way of registering and transferring ownership rights, whether for copyrights, currency, shares, or real-world or digital assets.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45170/45170-2.jpg"/></p>

<p>As <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=c1659f4532&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Christina Macedo</a>, founder of <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=8af8d9041b&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Ready Games Network</a>, told us in Cologne, "Gamers are starting to see the advantage of being the owners of their creations and data.”</p>

<p>“Web3 allows gamers to own their in-game assets across platforms, for the lifetime of those assets.”</p>

<p>“This, and the platform-independent ownership of a gamer's data kept in their personal wallets, is only just beginning to be explored."</p>

<p><strong>Web3 is all about giving users ownership and control over their data and assets.</strong></p>

<p>The number of iterations in Web3 business models is so similar to the early days of the internet, when companies like <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=f7b5d8e47b&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Webvan</a> and <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=6de297b51f&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Pets.com</a> failed in 2000 only to see the same business models become successful twenty years later when clients were ready.</p>

<p>So, just because early attempts at Web3 gaming failed doesn't mean that the advantages for gamers and content creators aren't convincing enough.</p>

<p>It simply means that <strong>game developers need to learn from the mistakes of the past and build better products.</strong></p>

<p>Sending emails from your Nokia communicator was no fun. The iPhone had to come along to get the user experience right.</p>

<h4>Portfolio Snapshot</h4>

<p>At Gamescom, we spent an hour with <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=f091bca165&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Carsten van Husen</a>, the CEO of our portfolio company <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=d41c16344e&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">ELEET Games</a>.</p>

<p>We have a particular interest in founders with a lot of industry experience, and Carsten fits our investment profile perfectly.</p>

<p>He is a veteran in the gaming industry, having served as the CEO of Karlsruhe-based <a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=85aa62e90d&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Gameforge</a> (a major German gaming success story) for ten years.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45170/45170-3.jpg"/></p>

<p>Carsten gave us a demo of "<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=5709a6b15d&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Oathbound</a>," the latest release from his studio, and explained his vision for Web3 gaming.</p>

<p>He believes that one of the key advantages of the Web3 architecture is the <strong>reduced customer acquisition costs</strong>, as the community can be financially incentivized through tokens.</p>

<p>Gaming used to be a hobby that was not monetizable.</p>

<p>However, in the Web3 universe, the trading of NFTs and revenue sharing creates an opportunity to not only enjoy the game but also make money from it.</p>

<p>Our takeaway from Gamescom is that the value proposition of Web3 and blockchain for the gaming industry and players is strong.</p>

<p>Digital ownership of assets and personal information, the opportunity to co-own the value of a community, and the portability of assets across platforms and games are all paradigm shifts that may be controversial now but will ultimately prevail because they provide undeniable benefits.</p>

<h4>Recommended</h4>

<p>I recently enjoyed reading the book "<a href="https://bfc.us8.list-manage.com/track/click?u=b24a4a180be6b26d2e7ed0cdb&amp;id=4ad35f412e&amp;e=d2cc07be1e" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Chip War: The Fight for the World's Most Critical Technology</a>” by Chris Miller.</p>

<p>It is an <strong>epic account of the decades-long battle to control microchip technology</strong>, the world's most critical resource.</p>

<p><img alt="newsletter_img"  loading="lazy" src="/images/news/45170/45170-4.png"/></p>

<p>The book explains:</p>

<ul><li>Why chip production moved to Asia early on (higher dexterity of Asian workers)</li><li>Why modern chip production is the single most complex production process in the world (replicating just one laser in an extreme ultra-violet system requires assembling 457,329 parts)</li><li>How the automotive chip shortage during the COVID-19 pandemic was entirely the fault of car companies (they canceled orders in anticipation of lower demand)</li><li>Why China should have no interest in making things complicated around Taiwan (because producing the latest chip technology in other parts of the world will not be an alternative for decades)</li></ul>

<p>The book is full of insights and analysis that will help you understand the chip industry and the global geopolitical landscape.</p>

<p>And I promise you that you will not be able to put it down until the very end.</p>
